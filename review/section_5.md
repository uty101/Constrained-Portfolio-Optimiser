## Deviations from PLAN.md

1. **Two Section 4 tests changed with decision 5.0.a.2.** `test_metrics_respect_ruin_truncation` asserted the ruined strategy's Sharpe over its path; it now asserts NaN and `ruin_month == "2020-03"`. `test_sharpe_diff_against_self_is_zero` asserted the ruined strategy's point Sharpe; it now asserts NaN with the interval. No tolerance was changed. `test_metrics_on_synthetic_series` also asserts `ruin_month` is NaN for a strategy that is not ruined.
2. **`ruin_month`** is the month (`YYYY-MM`) of the ruin decision date, the first month with `ruined = True`, and empty for a strategy that is not ruined. It sits after `ruined` in `metrics_all.csv`. The ruined text box entry in Chart 1 uses the same value.
3. **Chart 1:**
   - A negative dx in `LABEL_OFFSETS` right-aligns the label so it sits left of its point (`equal_weight` only).
   - A label that is not an allocator, which happens only in the synthetic test, takes the `mv_unconstrained` offset.
   - `plot_frontier` takes an optional `boxes` dict, filled with the rendered label boxes.
   - `write_charts` also returns Σ, the label boxes and the overlapping pairs.
4. **Step 5.1:**
   - The 4 μ-based strategies are labelled with registry-style ids for set B: `mv_constrained|lw_cc|sample|B`, `mv_constrained|lw_cc|bayes_stein|B` and `black_litterman|lw_cc|bl|B`. These ids are not in the registry.
   - The references use Σ_lw_cc (open decision 7).
   - The references are solved on every draw with the perturbed μ, so their 0 dispersion is measured, not assumed.
   - The momentum divisor is `bl.mom_lookback − bl.mom_skip` = 11 from `config.toml`, and the μ divisor is `sample.window_months` = 36.
   - Quantiles use `np.quantile` with numpy's default rule, as in step 4.4.
   - `frac_top_asset_changes` compares the argmax of the signed weights.
   - Each configured date is checked to be a decision date.
   - Chart 3 is a 4 × 2 grid with the 8th panel hidden. The p50 line is drawn inside each box.
5. **Step 5.2:**
   - `realised_net_ann_return` is over the whole wealth path, first month included, as `ann_return` in `metrics_all.csv`. The τ = 0.30 row therefore equals that strategy's `ann_return` there (0.0926190).
   - `mean_dual_bp_per_pct` is the mean of `turnover_dual` × 100 over every month after the first, slack months (dual 0) included. It is NaN for `none`.
   - `realised_net_vs_none_bp_pa` sits after `realised_net_ann_return`.
   - The `none` row has `tau` "none", and `n_binding` and `n_relaxed` 0.
   - `test_turnover_frontier_monotone_turnover` allows −1e-7, the step 3.4 turnover tolerance. Once the limit is slack, τ moves the solution only at solver precision: on the test panel, consecutive slack τ differ by up to 2.6e-9 in mean turnover, in both directions.
   - Chart 4 has free aspect. Return given up reaches 207 bp a year and cost saved only 6 bp, so equal axes squeezed every point into a vertical line. The region below the 45° line is shaded, and the 4 labels at the origin are stacked with leader lines.
6. **Step 5.3:**
   - The scale is applied to `one_way_bp` before the division by 1e4: c_i = (bp × scale)/1e4. Amendment 4.2.4 writes bp/1e4 × scale. The two are equal at scales 0 and 1, and the scale 1 run reproduces `periods.parquet` to 0.0. At scale 3 they may differ in the last bit.
